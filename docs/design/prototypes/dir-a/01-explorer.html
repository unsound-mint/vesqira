    Vesqira can refer to FITS files where they already live, or keep a managed copy. Nothing is read until you choose, and no file is changed either way.

    1. Register existing data. Give a path on this machine or an approved remote source. Vesqira reads the header, reports axes, units and WCS status, and shows a first plane without downloading the whole cube.

    3. Open it in the viewer, or pin it to a board for a supervisor.

    Remote sources that are temporarily unreachable appear in the list as ✕remote-OFFLINE with their last known header, never as missing files.
