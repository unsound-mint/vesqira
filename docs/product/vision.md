# Vision

**Vesqira** is an open-source, extensible workspace for scientific research. Tagline (provisional, not approved): *Your research, connected.*

## The problem

A researcher's evidence is scattered: FITS cubes on a cluster, result maps from a pipeline, figures in slides, and a supervisor who can neither open the data nor see how a figure was made. Sharing means screenshots that lose their provenance.

## What Vesqira does

Connects data, visualization, experiments, and review in one trustworthy place:

- Register existing data without copying it, and inspect it with honest metadata.
- Compare results with the right scientific semantics.
- Assemble source-linked figures and research boards.
- Let a supervisor review a stable, scoped snapshot with no cluster access.

## What makes it different

Scientific honesty is a product feature. Vesqira never invents calibration or coordinates, keeps axial orientations, masks, and missing values semantically distinct, labels provenance as verified / declared / inferred / unresolved, and shows a reviewer exactly what was published.

## Extensibility

The core stays discipline-neutral and small, with discipline-specific behavior behind narrow typed adapters. v0.1 ships astronomy only.
